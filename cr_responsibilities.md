# CS 7th Semester CR Responsibilities

## 1. Class Scheduling Aur Teacher Communication
* Rozana ka timetable set karwana aur manage karna.
* Har roz teachers se rabta karke confirm karna ke aaj unki class hogi ya nahi.
* Class ko daily basis par WhatsApp group mein class ki timings aur updates ka message karna.
* Teachers aur students ke darmian 99% communication sirf WhatsApp ke through maintain karna.
* Thursday ko ghar jane ke baad pichle dinon ka ya achanak aane wale departmental/teacher issues ko remotely handle karna jab aap campus mein physically mojood na hon.

## 2. Attendance Management (Haazri Ka Hisaab)
* Rozana classes aur papers ke doran attendance sheets par bachon ki haazri lagana.
* Sheets ki safed-poshi aur record-keeping (kuch teachers khud le jate hain aur kuch final exams tak aap ke paas chhor dete hain).
* Finals ke qareeb sab se mushkil kaam: Har single student ki 75% attendance calculate karna, clearance check karna, aur teachers ko verify karke dena.

## 3. Material Aur Assignment Logistics
* Course outlines, past papers, lecture slides, aur notes ko Google Drive link par upload karna aur sab tak seamlessly pohanchana.
* Teachers ke liye poori class se assignments collect karna, aur unhein proper sequence (roll number wise) mein lagana.
* Class ki presentations ke liye schedules banana aur unko smoothly execute karwana.

## 4. Students Queries Aur Group Management
* Har subject mein jo students ke groups bante hain projects ya presentations ke liye, unko manage karna.
* Pori class ke dheron students ki har tarah ki random queries, masail, aur sawalat ko sunna aur unka jawab dena.
